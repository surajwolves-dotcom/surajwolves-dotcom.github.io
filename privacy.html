Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

Animated Motivation is an animated short-story channel run by Suraj Singh in the United Kingdom. Contact: singh.sid91@gmail.com

What the publishing tool does

We run a private tool ("Animated Motivation Uploader") that publishes our own videos to our own YouTube channel and our own Instagram account. It is used only by the channel owner. It is not a public app and no one else can sign in to it.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): with the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and post a comment on our own videos. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.
  • Instagram (Meta): the tool uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels and a first comment to our own Instagram account.

The access tokens are stored encrypted as private secrets in our own automation account and are used only for the purposes above.

Data about viewers

We do not collect, store or sell personal information about people who watch our videos or visit this site. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. YouTube and Instagram process data under their own privacy policies.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Meta Business settings. When access is revoked, any stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
